About Us

Rupesh Bharat Joshi is the founding member of Swati Enterprises, an accomplished Electrical Engineering professional with over 20 years of experience in Design, Marketing and Concept-based Solution selling with a strong specialization in LV Switchboards and Power Quality Solutions.

Swarup means "Self Appearance" representing Authenticity, Originality, and complete Alignment with True Purpose.
